Ten years of building in Bahrain

We started in trading and contracting in 2016 and grew by being the contractor clients called back — first for industrial works, then for the interiors those clients wanted finished to a higher standard than the market was delivering.

Who we are

Excellence in design and delivery

Established in 2016, Golden Pearl has been serving the needs of the retail, commercial and residential construction sectors in Bahrain. Our ethos is excellence in design and delivery, working across architecture, master planning, interior design and graphic design.

We are well placed to offer speedy execution in civil works and interior decoration, with a large workforce of civil, interior and MEP workers. We have built and strengthened in-house capability for the supervision and execution of large turnkey jobs — partitions, panelling, false ceilings, furniture, and all interior-related civil, MEP and finishing works.

That range is why an industrial client such as Midal Cables can hand us a plant room and an office fit-out in the same year, and why Universal Rolling Mill appointed us as their permanent contractor for civil, MEP and interior works.

How we operate

Five principles we can be held to

Not values on a wall — the things a client can check we actually did.

Safety

Industrial clients audit safety before they audit price. We work to site-specific method statements and permit systems because on a live potline or cable line there is no acceptable margin for improvisation.

verified

Quality

Finishes are inspected against the approved sample, not against opinion. Where we fabricate the joinery ourselves, the standard is set before anything reaches site.

gavel

Transparency

Variations get priced and agreed before the work happens, not presented at the final account. If a programme is slipping, the client hears it from us first.

groups

Our people

Directly employed trades rather than a rotating pool. Consistency of finish across fifteen villas or three café branches depends on the same hands doing the work.

Repeat business

Four separate packages for Midal between 2021 and 2023, and two in the same tower for Al Zamil. Being asked back is the measure that matters.

Retail & showroom work

Where industrial discipline meets a shopfront

Retail is the least forgiving work we do. A mall handover date does not move, the finish is inches from a customer's face, and the brand standard is set elsewhere and cannot be negotiated.

Our automotive showroom work runs from structural glazing and composite cladding on the façade through to the timber-panelled display walls and product plinths inside. The same discipline applies to Godiva at Al A'ali Mall, the Al Zamil Group headquarters, and five F&B brands rolled out across Bahrain's malls and towers.

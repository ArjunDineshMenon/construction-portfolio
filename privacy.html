Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy describes the current behaviour of this website. It has not been reviewed by a legal practitioner. Have it checked against Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 30 of 2018) before relying on it.

Who we are

Golden Pearl Trading & Contracting is a construction and contracting company registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Golden Pearl Trading & Contracting, and "the site" means this website.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into an enquiry form on this site. That is:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • The discipline you selected
  • The project details you wrote

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics tracking, or any form of visitor profiling on this site. We do not collect payment information.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry and, if it leads to work, to administer that project. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored using Supabase, a hosted database service, which may process and store the data on servers outside Bahrain. Fonts and stylesheets on this site are served by Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which will receive your IP address as a normal part of serving those files.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are retained while they are commercially relevant — typically for the duration of any resulting project plus the defects liability period, and for as long as we are required to keep project records.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details on our contact page and we will respond as promptly as we reasonably can.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

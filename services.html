Capabilities

Six disciplines, delivered in-house

Structure, building services and finishes under one contract — so programme, quality and responsibility sit with a single team rather than being split between subcontractors.

How we work

From enquiry to handover

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You send drawings, a scope, or simply the problem. We tell you quickly whether it is work we should be pricing.

  2. 02

    Site survey

    We measure on site and confirm access, services and constraints — including shutdown windows on live industrial sites.

  3. 03

    Drawings & quotation

    2D layouts or 3D perspectives where they help, with a priced scope and a programme you can hold us to.

  4. 04

    Execution

    One site team across civil, MEP, joinery and finishes, with a named point of contact and progress you can verify.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Snagging closed out, documentation issued, and a defects liability period we actually answer during.

Discipline 02

MEP & Fire Systems

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire systems are where a building either works for decades or generates complaints from the first week. We design and install them to be accurate on day one and maintainable afterwards.

Our engineers model MEP services and review them for constructability, efficiency and access for maintenance — across commercial buildings, hospitals, industrial facilities and high-specification commercial kitchens.

  • check_circleHVAC design and installation, including heavy-duty industrial systems
  • check_circleElectrical distribution, lighting and containment
  • check_circlePlumbing, drainage and water treatment
  • check_circleFire detection and suppression system design

Representative work: Universal Rolling Mill MEP and civil package, Al Shaya warehouse HVAC, Florist Daughter integrated planting MEP.
